The problem

Why Indian factories are still
working in Excel.

SAP costs ₹25 lakhs to implement. Generic software does not understand Indian manufacturing. So factories use Excel — and lose margin on every quote.

❌
Manual cost sheets take hours
Every enquiry means rebuilding the same Excel from scratch. When steel prices change, every sheet needs updating manually. One missed update and you quote at a loss.
⚠️
EU buyers now demand carbon data
CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism) is in force since January 2026. If you cannot provide your carbon footprint per tonne, your European buyer pays 3× more in carbon tax — and they take it out of your price.
🔧
Machine breakdowns are unpredictable
There is no early warning system. Vibration graphs sit on a screen that only a specialist can read. A bearing fails at 2am and your production stops. Nobody saw it coming.
